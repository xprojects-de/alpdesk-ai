# Code of conduct

## What it is about

Be kind to each other, help each other, and never treat anyone badly for who they are.

In practice: we treat each other with respect regardless of age, origin, gender, sexual orientation, disability,
appearance, religion or experience. Criticism is aimed at the work, not at the person. Whoever asks a question that
seems obvious to others gets an answer, not a jibe.

Not acceptable in particular: insulting or demeaning remarks, sexualised language or advances, harassment inside the
project or outside of it, publishing other people's private data without their consent, and persistent disruption.

## Where it applies

In every part of the project - issues, pull requests, commit messages, discussions - and whenever someone represents
the project in public.

## When something happens

Report it to `infos@x-projects.de`. Reports are treated confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and, for repeated or serious misconduct, exclude people from the
project for a time or for good. Decisions are explained where possible.

## Origin

These rules follow the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, but are
deliberately shorter and in our own words. In doubt, the spirit counts, not the letter.
